Release limits on the S1, S2, S3 line

Every plan runs on the same 100 eight-hour shifts in FlowSim.

The line

Every order visits S1, then S2, then S3. Orders wait in the backlog until they are released into the shop.

StationAverage minutes per jobCapacity per hourLoad

  • Orders arrive about every 7.5 minutes, so 60 ÷ 7.5 = 8 orders per hour.
  • Load is orders per hour ÷ capacity. S2 has the lowest capacity, so it is the bottleneck.
  • The gaps between orders and the processing times are random (exponential).
  • A rack is the waiting space in front of a station. When the rack after a station is full, that station is blocked. It holds its finished job and cannot start the next one.
  • Under push, the racks have no limit, so no station is ever blocked.
  • Orders stop arriving after 8 hours, and the rest finish in overtime.

Your task

Your plant manager wants minutes in the shop at 30 or less. Find the plan that meets this target with the fewest minutes from order.

  • Minutes in the backlog run from the order's arrival to its release into the shop.
  • Minutes in the shop run from release to finish.
  • Minutes from order run from arrival to finish. This is the time the customer feels.
